# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

App Name: Summa Theologica

Developer/Entity: Kenneth Emeka Odoh

---

Overview
Summa Theologica ("we," "our," or "us") is built with an uncompromising commitment to user privacy. We believe that your personal data belongs to you, and you alone. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices, confirming our policy of zero data collection.

---

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information or user data.

* No Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) to use Summa Theologica.
* Local-First Architecture: All app data, configurations, and content operate entirely and locally on your device. Nothing you input, read, or save within the application is transmitted to us or any third party.
* No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use tracking pixels, cookies, telemetry tools, or third-party analytics SDKs to monitor your behavior, usage patterns, or device identifiers.

---

2. Device Permissions
Summa Theologica may request specific device permissions strictly to deliver core functionality locally on your device:

* Storage / File System: Used exclusively to read and write local application files (such as offline databases or downloaded content packages) directly on your device storage. This data never leaves your device.

We do not use these permissions to harvest contacts, location data, media files, or any other private information.

---

3. Third-Party Services

Because Summa Theologica functions entirely offline or independently without cloud synchronization:

* We do not integrate third-party advertising networks.
* We do not share data with data brokers, marketers, or external services.

---

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any data from any user—regardless of age—Summa Theologica is completely safe for children and fully complies with children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

---

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted directly within this document with an updated effective date. Because we do not collect user contact information, we encourage you to review this policy periodically.
